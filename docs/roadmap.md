# Roadmap

Murder on Record is built in six stages. Each stage builds on the previous one.

1. **Landing and case selection** (done). Visual foundation, entry screen, opening sound sequence, call dialogue and the three-tape selection screen.
2. **Case file for Tape No. 1.** The dossier: circumstances of the murder, how the victim was found, crime scene details and initial evidence. Clues that stay locked until the player asks the right question.
3. **Suspect files.** Five suspects, each with a photo or illustration, personal details and relationship to the victim.
4. **Interview system.** Around ten selectable questions, a total budget of six questions across all suspects, and one pre-recorded answer per suspect and question.
5. **Deduction checklist.** Investigator notes in the style of Clue to confirm or eliminate the weapon, the location, access to the scene and alibis.
6. **Ending and polish.** Accusation, outcome, sound tuning, accessibility, performance and publishing.
